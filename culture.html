Barranquilla · Caribbean coast

Carnival is a whole city joining in.

The Carnival of Barranquilla fills the four days before Lent with dance, music, costumes, and playful pageantry. Its traditions carry influences from Indigenous, African, and European communities, shaped into something unmistakably local.
